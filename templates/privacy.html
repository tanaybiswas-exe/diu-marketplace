Privacy Policy & Data Governance

At DIU Smart Marketplace, we highly value your privacy. This document outlines how we collect, safeguard, and process user credentials and listing data.

  • Identity Protection: We only authenticate verified @diu.edu.bd domains to ensure zero external compromise.
  • Data Retention: Active product photos uploaded via ImgBB and user communication channels remain secure and are subject to immediate removal upon deletion by the listing user.
  • IP Logs: Security logs and IP tracking are restricted to platform anti-fraud compliance and policy enforcement.
